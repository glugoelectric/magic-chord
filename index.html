    How to write a song

    Put chords on their own line, spaced so each chord sits over the word where it changes. Blank lines split the song into blocks; a block never splits across columns. Section names in brackets show as small labels.

    [Chorus]
C            Am
Every line of words
F                G
gets a chord line above

    Pasting from Ultimate Guitar works as-is. ChordPro inline format like [G]Words [C]here is converted automatically.
